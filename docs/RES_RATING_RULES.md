# RES Rating Rules (Source of Truth)

> Only authorised checklist structure is stored. The full IGBC copyrighted guide is NOT reproduced.

## IGBC Green New Buildings v4 — Commercial
Category max points (Owner-Occupied / Tenant-Occupied):
- Site Selection and Planning: 15 / 15
- Water Conservation: 20 / 20
- Energy Efficiency: 26 / 29
- Building Materials and Resources: 13 / 13
- Indoor Environmental Quality: 15 / 12
- Innovation and Decarbonisation: 11 / 11
- **Total: 100 / 100**

Certification thresholds (total points):
- < 50 → Uncertified
- 50–59 → Certified
- 60–69 → Silver
- 70–79 → Gold
- 80–100 → Platinum

Rules: mandatory criteria must all pass or project is Not Eligible; score cannot exceed category/template max; thresholds come from the assigned version. Do NOT apply these categories to Residential/Hotel/Hospital — those use their own templates (Phase 2, authorised data).

Project type → template map: Commercial → IGBC Green New Buildings; Residential → IGBC Green Homes; Hotel → IGBC Green Hotels; Hospital → IGBC Green Healthcare (Residential/Hotel/Hospital templates pending authorised data → project allowed but shows "checklist under configuration", no fake score).
